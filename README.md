# VATS

**Visual Assisted Targeting System** for Cyberpunk 2077 (game 2.5): while scanning, pick an enemy and one of its body parts, then hit that part with a quickhack or the smart gun, in deep slow motion.

**Status: in research.** Nothing to install yet. The research spikes run in [REDnode](https://github.com/Edgemodders/REDnode)'s VATS spike app; this repo holds VATS's design and its own scripts as they are built.

## What it is

A drop-in [REDnode](https://github.com/Edgemodders/REDnode) mod. It needs REDnode (and what REDnode needs: RED4ext, redscript), nothing else. Its quickhacks are made with [EDGEhack](https://github.com/Edgemodders/EDGEhack), the quickhack editor, but VATS does not depend on EDGEhack to run.
